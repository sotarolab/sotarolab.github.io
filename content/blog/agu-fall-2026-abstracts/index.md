I am excited to share that I submitted two first-author abstracts for the AGU Fall Meeting 2026:

- [Downscaling CMIP6 projections of weather extremes over Chile: a CNN super-resolution approach](/publications/agu2026-cmip6-downscaling-chile/)
- [LSTM and diffusion models compared with process-based and operational benchmarks during atmospheric-river floods in US West Coast basins](/publications/agu2026-lstm-diffusion-ar-floods/)

<!--more-->

The first trains a convolutional neural network to downscale CMIP6-based
projections of extreme precipitation and temperature over Chile's complex
topography. The second stress-tests LSTM and diffusion streamflow models
against process-based benchmarks (GR4J, CemaNeige) during atmospheric-river
flood events in US West Coast basins and evaluates their performance based on their hydrological regimes.
