A live nowcast of landfalling atmospheric rivers along the Chilean coast. Real-time GEFS ensemble forecasts of integrated vapor transport (IVT) are categorized on the [Ralph et al. (2019)](https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-18-0023.1) AR scale, combining IVT magnitude with duration to distinguish beneficial rainfall from hazardous flooding.

Operated publicly through a **Category 4 landfalling AR event**, with a live observation ingestion pipeline running across Chile.

<!--more-->

Atmospheric rivers deliver a large share of Chile's cool-season precipitation, and the same events drive its worst flooding — the difference between the two is mostly duration, which is exactly what the AR scale encodes and what a raw precipitation forecast does not.

*Live dashboard is public; source repository is private.*
